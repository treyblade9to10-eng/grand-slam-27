# MLB Grand Slam 27

A 3D baseball game that runs in the browser (phone, Chromebook, PC, controller).

- All 30 clubs with real 40-man rosters (as of Nov 10, 2026), real 2026 stats and real pitch mixes.
- All 30 real ballparks built from their real outfield distances and wall heights (Fenway's Monster, Wrigley's ivy, Oracle's water in right, Kauffman's fountains, Coors' thin air and more).
- Play Now, Season (20 to 162 games, real 12-team playoffs), and Home Run Derby.
- Broadcast-style presentation: stadium flyover, lineups, pitching matchup, scorebug, lower thirds, replays of the big moments on the video board.

Fan project, not affiliated with MLB or any club. Team badges are original designs, not the clubs' trademarked logos.
